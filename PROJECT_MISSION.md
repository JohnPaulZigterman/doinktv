# DoinkTV Mission

DoinkTV is a Patreon-backed underground TV station performed like a live instrument.

The station should feel alive even when nobody is touching it: scheduled programming, Internet Archive discovery, generated bumps, supporter picks, chat, voting, and Ableton-style live FX all feeding one coherent broadcast experience.

## Product Spine

1. Performance rack core
   - Make the admin feel like they are playing the channel, not operating a form.
   - Prioritize rack units, macros, scene launches, clean-signal escape, active/decay state, and reusable presets.

2. Continuity engine
   - Make the station trustworthy.
   - Explain why something played, what will play next, when filler is intentional, and how scheduled programming outranks the live queue.

3. Block identity system
   - Treat every recurring block like a channel inside the channel.
   - Give each block a scene, color, bump package, chaos ceiling, and recurring rituals.

4. Community programming loop
   - Make viewer and Patreon crew input visibly matter.
   - Track picks from suggestion to approval, queue, airing, credit, and future voting.

5. Media quality and safety
   - Keep automation from serving broken, low-fit, or explicitly pornographic material.
   - Centralize Archive scoring, filters, captions, language hints, duration rules, and rejection memory.

6. Codebase operability
   - Preserve momentum by splitting growing monoliths into clear feature modules.
   - Add behavioral tests around scheduling, queue protection, FX state, community votes, and Archive search.

## Maintenance Truth

The concept is strong enough to justify tightening the architecture now. The biggest risk is not missing features; it is letting powerful features stay scattered across oversized files until every change becomes spooky. The next phase should keep shipping visible magic while carving out clean ownership boundaries.

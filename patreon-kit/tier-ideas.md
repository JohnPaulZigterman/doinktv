# Tier Ideas

Keep tiers simple at launch. The vibe should feel like supporting a station, not buying a complicated software subscription.

## $3 - Signal Helper

For people who want to keep the weird little station alive.

Includes:
- Patron-only station updates
- Programming notes and behind-the-scenes posts
- Occasional polls on upcoming blocks and experiments

## $5 - Control Room

For viewers who want a little more say in what DoinkTV becomes.

Includes:
- Everything in Signal Helper
- Priority polls for block themes and station features
- Early looks at new bumps, UI experiments, and schedule ideas

## $10 - Broadcast Menace

For people who want the channel to get stranger on purpose.

Includes:
- Everything in Control Room
- Suggest a bump prompt, block theme, or weird programming lane
- Name-in-the-credits style thank-you posts or occasional on-site supporter shoutouts if you want them

## Optional Higher Tier - Station Sponsor

Only add this if you want a higher support option without promising too much custom work.

Includes:
- Everything above
- A periodic supporter-coded station ID, bumper phrase, or “this block brought to you by” gag

Avoid promising:
- Exact airtime guarantees
- Custom programming rights
- Anything requiring rights clearance unless you already have it handled

# Patreon Page Copy

## Short Intro

DoinkTV is a strange little internet TV station: cartoons, odd public-domain finds, music blocks, station breaks, viewer chaos, and lovingly broken broadcast nonsense.

Supporting the Patreon helps keep the station running, growing, and getting weirder in ways that actually make the channel better: cleaner scheduling, custom bumps, better media sourcing, more themed blocks, and more tools for the live TV party.

## About Section

DoinkTV is built like a pirate broadcast that found a web server and decided to become everyone’s favorite questionable late-night channel.

The goal is simple: make a 24/7-ish hangout stream that feels programmed, alive, and deeply handmade. The schedule rotates through cartoons, retro oddities, music blocks, talk-show energy, Internet Archive discoveries, special bumps, station IDs, viewer votes, and the occasional technical decision that probably should not have been made but somehow rules.

Patreon support helps pay for the boring parts that let the fun parts shine: hosting, storage, tooling, maintenance time, asset work, and all the strange connective tissue that turns a queue of videos into an actual station.

If you like the feeling of stumbling onto a channel that should not exist but absolutely does, you are in the right place.

## Short Support Pitch

Help keep the signal warm. Patreon support goes toward hosting, station tools, custom bumps, better programming blocks, and the ongoing project of making DoinkTV feel less like a playlist and more like a living, slightly unlicensed broadcast dimension.
